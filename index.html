About Phasebridge

We work where
disciplines overlap.

A team with genuine depth across the chemistry–biology boundary — computational, synthetic, biological, industrial. Not assembled for the brief. Built this way.

Biological Capability

Our biological work — bacterial assays, cell-based assays, microbial testing, and time-kill studies — is conducted under CDC-certified biosafety protocols, with proper containment and documented procedures.

Phasebridge grew from a single observation: most scientific problems that stall don't stall because the answer doesn't exist — they stall because the people who could find it aren't in the room.

Our team spans end-to-end drug discovery, organic and process chemistry, water technology, biological sciences, and procurement. That range isn't accidental. It's what lets us take on problems that cross disciplines and deliver results that single-domain teams can't.

We work across pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, oil and gas, water treatment, and electrochemical technologies — wherever a complex technical problem needs someone to stop advising and start doing.
